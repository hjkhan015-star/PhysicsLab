# Class 8 improvement roadmap

Phases 1 and 2 are done. Phase 3 is planned from the teacher's request list.

## Phase 1 — DONE
- **Force and Pressure** — three header tabs. `force8`: Contact (push, pull, lift, slide), Non-contact (magnet attract/repel, rubbed comb, gravity), Effects (shape, direction, start, speed up, slow down), with a force slider (more force → more effect). `pressure8`: five base materials × six forces × four areas, with pressure shown in kPa/MPa. `liquidpressure8`: tank (holes at different depths), cylinder (equal push in all directions), dam (why the base is wide), and a tap that holds the water level or lets it drain.
- **Friction** — water / oil / powder / rollers on four surfaces, a live zoom of the surface bumps, static vs sliding vs rolling friction, and a heat glow.
- **Sound** — audio rebuilt (starts on first tap, follows the sliders live, harmonics so low notes are audible on phones, mutes on Pause); only the vibrating part moves (speaker cone, fork prongs, string); medium selector (air / water / empty space).
- Engine: `tone`, new `beep`, header `tabs`, `hidden` syllabus entries, test harness now sweeps every mode.

## Phase 2 — Electricity, Natural phenomena, Light — DONE
- **Chemical Effects of Current** — `electro8`: the circuit now shows a battery (+/− ends), a hinged **key (switch)** you open and close (an open key means no current, no bulb, no plating) and the bulb; a **battery slider 1.5–9 V** (current = volts × how well the liquid conducts; bulb, bubbles and plating rate follow it). Plating runs in real time on a fast clock (1 s = 3 min): the copper plate is a grid of tiles that shrink and fall away, copper bits drift across, and copper tiles grow on the object. Monitor: current, bulb, time, mg lost by the plate, mg gained by the object (equal). The plated item is now called **Object** so it is not confused with the key (switch).
- **Natural Phenomena** — `lightning8`: **rubbing in real time** — a hand with a wool cloth sweeps over both balloons at the chosen speed (Stop … Very fast); minus signs fly from the cloth to the balloon and pile up, plus signs gather on the cloth, a charge meter fills, paper bits jump once the charge is high, and *Touch to discharge* earths everything. Storm (renamed from Thunderstorm): lumpy dark clouds with charge signs, rain, house with roof and lit windows, jagged **branching bolts**, a flash overlay (at most 2 pulses per strike, strikes at least 3 s apart), a **You are 1–4 km** slider with a sound ring and stopwatch (3 s per km), and an optional thunder sound (`rumble` in `scene.js`).
- **Light** — `reflection8`: **Mirror tilt** slider (−30° to +30°; reflected ray turns twice as much); ray names (incident ray, reflected ray, normal) with i and r arcs and labels; a real **faceted rough wall** (each ray reflects off the bump it hits, with a second bounce when it hits the next bump); **One / Three torches** (parallel rays: the mirror keeps them parallel, the wall scatters them); one arrowhead per ray, at its far end only (`ARROWS` constant switches them off). `eye8`: Monitor now shows pupil width (mm) and light let in (%).
- Engine: `rumble()` thunder sound in `scene.js`. Test harness unchanged.

## Phase 3a — Earth, Moon, Sun and planets — DONE
- **`stars8` rebuilt** (tab *Earth*): **Day** mode — a spinning Earth lit by the Sun (hard day/night line), a red pin ("You") that travels from day into night, time-of-day slider with clock times, *Run time* switch; Monitor shows day/night, time and Sun height. **Year** mode — Earth goes round the Sun with the axis tilted 23.5° (always the same way), date slider (5-day steps), the four key dates (21 Mar, 21 Jun, 22 Sep, 21 Dec) drawn as ghost Earths, place slider (South 30° / Equator / North 26°); Monitor shows the season where you live, day length, Sun height at noon and the Sun distance (to show that distance is not the cause). Caption says the tilt, not the distance, causes seasons.
- **`eclipse8` (new, tab *Eclipses*)**: **Solar**, **Lunar** and **Phases**. Real shadow geometry from the drawn sizes: dark shadow (umbra), light shadow (penumbra) and the extension past the umbra tip; *Moon moves* slider (0° = line-up); *Moon is near* switch (near → total, far → annular); *Tilt Moon's orbit* switch plus *Turn the tilt* slider (nodes) to show why there is no eclipse every month; a **View from Earth** picture (Sun, Moon, corona, ring, red Moon, lit part of the Moon). Monitor shows eclipse type, % of Sun covered / % of Moon in the dark shadow, gap from the line, day of the month, % lit. Safety note in the caption, a label and the picture.
- **`planets8` (new, tab *Planets*)**: eight planets round the Sun with dotted orbits; views **All** (orbits squeezed), **Inner** and **Outer** (distances in true proportion); Time Stop / Slow / Fast so inner planets visibly go faster; pick a planet for a one-line fact; Monitor: order, inner/outer, distance (million km), year length, speed (km/s), time passed. Asteroid belt drawn in the All view.
- Engine: new shared `earth.js` (crude world map drawn in code, `litMat` day/night sphere, `ll()` lon/lat helper); `tests/check.py` now also flips every switch in each mode and re-checks the dock (a switch can reveal another slider).

## Phase 3b — Sky objects, scale and satellites — DONE (second tab group: *Sky / Scale / Satellites*, home card `skyobjects8`)
- **`skyobjects8`**: **Comet** — Kepler ellipse (faster near the Sun), ion + dust tail always pointing away from the Sun, growing as it nears the Sun and shrinking as it leaves; Earth's path drawn for comparison; Monitor: tail word (none / short / long / very long), distance, speed, orbit time. **Meteor** — a rock enters the air: *meteoroid* (space) → *meteor* (glow, shooting star) → *meteorite* (lands); rock size (Pebble / Stone / Boulder) and speed decide whether anything lands; names highlighted at the right stage; no flashing (smooth glow). **Stars** — chart looking north: Saptarishi (Big Dipper) with the two pointer stars and line to the Pole Star, Cassiopeia, background stars, the sky turning round the Pole Star through the night.
- **`scale8`**: one log-scale zoom slider (Earth → Moon → Sun → planets → nearest star → Milky Way → galaxies → edge of the Universe) with smooth zoom, labels that avoid each other, a red *You are here* pin, a scale bar, and the Monitor showing light-travel time, distance and view width. "Sizes true for this zoom only" label; tiny things are enlarged dots.
- **`satellites8`**: **Orbits** (low: ISS 400 km + an Earth-watching polar satellite; *High orbits* switch: GPS, geostationary with a marker that stays above one spot, and the Moon as the natural satellite) and **ISS map** (flat world map, ground track on/off, time slider + speed slider, ±51.6° lines). Model only: labelled "not live tracking, works offline".
- Shared: `earth.js` now also exports `frus()` (see-through cone used for shadows and comet tails).
